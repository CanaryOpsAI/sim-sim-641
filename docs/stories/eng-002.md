# CMP-1460 — Photo storage provisioning: regional buckets, retention lifecycle and the signed-URL issuer

Provision the two regional buckets the upload path writes to, with
KMS keys per region, lifecycle rules (raw originals kept 90 days,
derived thumbnails one year, both purged on a customer deletion
request) and the small service that issues 15-minute signed upload
URLs to the client. Nothing customer-facing; without it STORY-004's
resumable upload has nowhere safe to land and STORY-003's captures
cannot leave the device.

Acceptance (execution-specific):
- Buckets exist in both regions from infrastructure code, lifecycle
  rules verified by a test object ageing out of a short-TTL staging
  bucket.
- Signed URLs expire at 15 minutes and are rejected afterwards;
  access reviewed with Security.
